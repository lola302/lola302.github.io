First Chair

I built this app for myself to get ready for ski season. Every day it gives me a training session and habits to tick off, and it has a calendar where each day is graded like a ski run, notes so I can see how I'm improving, and extra workouts for when I want to do more.

I'm building a glove that will let me control my computer completely while sitting comfortably on my hand. The electronics already work, so I can move the cursor by tilting my hand and click or scroll by bending my fingers, all over Bluetooth, and now I'm working on turning it into an actual glove.
